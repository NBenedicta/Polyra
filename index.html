What you can do with Polyra

  • Translate 25 languages

    Type or paste text and Polyra detects the language for you, from Spanish and Japanese to Igbo and Swahili.

  • Speak instead of typing

    Tap the mic and talk. Your words are written out and translated.

  • Hear the pronunciation

    Press the speaker to have any text read aloud.

  • Have a conversation

    Two people, two languages. Take turns talking and Polyra interprets out loud.

  • Pick formal or casual

    Choose the right tone for French, German, Spanish and more.

  • Keep your favourites

    Recent translations are kept in your browser. Star the ones you'll need again.
